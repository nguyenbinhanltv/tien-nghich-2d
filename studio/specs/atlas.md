# Atlas tab behaviours

- **ATLAS-001** (staging) Packing writes beside the shipped atlas and compares with it; every shipped file is left untouched.
- **ATLAS-002** (staging) A packer that writes into a shipped directory and then fails is reported as a shipped change, not as an ordinary failure.
- **ATLAS-003** (staging) The handoff package is written only under the library's handoff directory, is blocked while an asset's recorded output licence does not match the project's stated profile, and never publishes or uploads anything.
- **ATLAS-004** New submissions record the art-bible version they were made under; when the rules change the studio only counts stale assets, and it never rewrites the records of an asset that has no stamp.
- **ATLAS-005** The disclosure drafts for Steam and itch.io state counts taken from the ledger and list what a person must decide, in English and Vietnamese, and draw no legal conclusion.
