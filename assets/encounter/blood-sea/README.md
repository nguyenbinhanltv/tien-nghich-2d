# The Drowned Court

A local Blood Sea encounter starring Vương Lâm in his Huyết Hải Trúc Cơ form and Oán Hồn Quỷ Vương. The sealed court and this duel are original adaptations of the Blood Sea material in `docs/LORE_ANALYSIS.md`. The player carries the manifest's soul banner and the boss's mask, spectral claws and jade soul fire distinguish his silhouette from the red-robed player.

## Play and inspect

From the repository root, run `node preview-app/serve.mjs --port 4173` and open `http://127.0.0.1:4173/preview-app/blood-sea/index.html`. Enter battle, move with WASD or the arrows, hold J to strike toward the king, press Space to dodge in your last movement direction, and press P to pause. Touch devices have direction, strike and dodge buttons. Each spirit blade travels 105 native pixels. A timed dodge through an impact shortens the next dodge cooldown.

The Bestiary's **Inspect poses** link opens this encounter's collection in the existing inspector. Select either actor, choose the clip and direction, pause, step, or enable the pivot overlay. The navigation link **Asset inspector** opens the full project collection.

**Game timing** plays the encounter clips with the same holds used by combat. The transport shows each frame's event and duration. For example, the player winds up for 150 ms, releases the blade for a 190 ms impact pose, then recovers for 90 ms. Pause and step to see instantaneous start markers. The speed selector also works with timed clips. Turn off Game timing to inspect at a fixed FPS.

The bead closes above Vương Lâm when a warning covers his position. The gold **DODGE** cue marks the final 280 ms before impact. Dodging through the blow opens a 900 ms **STRIKE** window. The first strike begun in that window deals two damage if it connects. Results count enemy impacts separately from health lost. An untouched clear requires zero hits taken. Fight time includes hitstop and excludes pauses and the victory animation. The fastest clear is stored for this ruleset in the local browser, with an in-memory fallback when storage is unavailable.

Expand **Art source and prompt** below an actor’s frame controls to read its exact request, inspect the input hash or open the local original. The prompt can be read with **Source crop** off.

Codex authored the combat effects, touch controls and interface. Its first pass identified the lateral north strike, east strike clearance and north pivot drift that led to the revised player export.

## Normalize and export

Run `python tools/scripts/build_blood_sea.py` with Pillow installed. Use `--check` to rebuild in a temporary directory and compare the assembly, rectangles, ground pivots and decoded runtime pixels. Verification also checks pinned inputs, manifest directions and dimensions, nonempty poses and a transparent margin around each frame.

- Source crop bounds start at the top left and exclude their right and bottom edges. The north correction is composited from its recorded rectangle into one cleared cell. The player’s original front and side banner cloth is masked and layered behind three action poses using the recorded polygons, angles and placements. The assembled result is `source/wl_blood_foundation.png`.
- Each pose's measured ground point maps to the same destination point. Actor-wide scales preserve relative reach across poses. Player frames use 32 × 32 cells with pivot (16, 30). Boss frames use 80 × 80 cells with pivot (40, 75).
- Sprites use nearest-neighbor sampling, alpha threshold 128, the project palette and a one-pixel ink outline. The player's 32px grade lifts dark cloth through a fixed gamma curve and reserves the darkest ink for a four-neighbor exterior contour. `bladeFacets` records a hilt-to-tip line in native pixel coordinates for each pose. The bronze glint is clipped to the original sword silhouette. The court is box-filtered once at export to 384 × 256. At runtime the canvas uses integer zoom and nearest-neighbor rendering. Narrow screens crop the court around the fight.
- Each atlas uses the existing `characters/<id>/<animation>_<direction>_<index>` key convention. The four player directions are independently drawn. The boss has south and east sources with a horizontally mirrored west view. Clip keys reference existing rectangles. There are 22 distinct key poses, plus sequence aliases. The broader manifest's animation totals describe the full game assets.
- The `timingMs` values and named phase sequences in the contract generate both the inspector's atlas timelines and `web/timing.mjs`, which drives the live encounter. A timing change takes effect in both after rebuilding.
- Supplementary sheets inherit the parent actor's size, directions, ground pivot, material grade and scale. Their canonical keys must be unique across atlas pages. Vương Lâm's 16 reaction poses supplement the original 22 poses across both actors. Dodge starts with a 60 ms push-off and 120 ms slide. On defeat he recoils for 150 ms and kneels for 410 ms before the results appear. His tucked blade is occluded in the rear-view slide.
- `web/` contains only runtime images and frame data. Browsers do not download ImageGen's high-resolution inputs to play.

## Regression checks

Pause or step to a player or boss pose, then use **Open pose link** to open that frame in another local tab. You can also copy the link from its context menu. It records the actor, clip, facing and one-based frame number, including manually stepped poses. The receiving tab opens paused. Enable the source comparison there to load the sheet. An incomplete or outdated link shows a notice and opens the first court actor paused.

The pose inspector redraws on frame and control changes. With the source crop open on the dash clip, paired one-second Chromium samples showed 122 native draws and zero source draws while paused on `156ed74`, and zero draws or animation callbacks after the fix. During playback, 12 measured pose transitions produced 12 native and 12 source draws. The full tile-sheet view also sleeps until a tile or control changes.

The court stops its animation loop while paused, after victory or defeat, and on the ready screen with reduced motion enabled. Resize and visibility restoration redraw the current state. Paired one-second Chromium samples recorded 121 ready and 122 paused callbacks and full background draws on `b491c07`, and zero in both states after the change. The browser suite also checks live motion-preference changes, score retention on resize and replay.

On the touch pad, press an arrow and slide the same thumb to turn. The diagonal sectors light two arrows. The center stops movement. Leaving the pad releases that contact until the next press. A second thumb can hold Strike or tap Dodge independently.

For an assembled delivery, run `python tools/scripts/build_blood_sea.py --check-runtime PATH/TO/web`. This read-only check works without the source images. To review motion at native scale, open **Inspect poses**, select an actor and a multi-frame clip, then step forward and enable **Previous pose**. The optional **Sound** button enables seven original procedural cues. The closing gold seal rings once per threat, a successful dodge answers with a rising pair of notes, and a connected counter adds a low bronze impact. Hurt, awakening and both endings have their own motifs. Codex authored the pitches, intervals and envelopes in `preview-app/blood-sea/sound.mjs`. The browser synthesizes them after the player enables sound.
